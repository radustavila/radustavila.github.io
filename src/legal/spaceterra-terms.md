## 1. The app

SpaceTerra shows the universe and Earth from public data: astronomy pictures, the sky over your place (sky map,
AR view, sky events, satellite passes, comets, the Milky Way), launches, space weather and aurora, earthquakes,
wildfires and other natural events, climate data, maps of Earth and the Moon, news and a daily quiz. It works
offline from what it has already downloaded.

The app has no accounts: no registration, no username, no password. It is free to use and shows banner ads.

SpaceTerra is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or any other data provider. Their names appear only to
credit where the data comes from.

Everything in the app is for interest and education. It comes from third-party sources and the app's own
calculations, may be late, incomplete or wrong, and may be shown from an older copy while you are offline.

- **Do not use the app to make safety decisions.** Earthquake, wildfire and natural-event data, earthquake alerts,
